Pawse Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 5, 2026

Overview

Pawse is a couples presence app that helps partners spend quality time together. Your privacy matters, and we've built Pawse to keep your data on your device.

Information We Collect

Stored on your device only:

  • Your bear's name (chosen by you)
  • Session durations and total presence time
  • Unlocked creatures and progress
  • App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device unless you use the partner linking feature described below.

Partner Linking (optional, via Apple CloudKit):

If you choose to link with a partner, we store the following in Apple's CloudKit public database:

  • A randomly generated 6-character invite code
  • Two anonymous iCloud record identifiers (assigned by Apple, not your name or email)
  • Link status (waiting or linked)

These identifiers are anonymous system IDs assigned by Apple. We never see, store, or transmit your name, email address, Apple ID, or any other personal information.

Information We Don't Collect

  • No email addresses
  • No real names or personal identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contacts or photos
  • No browsing or usage analytics
  • No crash reports
  • No advertising identifiers

We don't use analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or any third-party data collection frameworks.

Third-Party Services

Pawse uses Apple CloudKit solely for the optional partner linking feature. CloudKit is Apple's own cloud infrastructure and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. No other third-party services are used.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you use partner linking, the anonymous linking data described above is stored in Apple's CloudKit public database.

You can delete all local data by deleting the app. Partner linking records expire automatically.

In-App Purchases

Pawse offers a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Pawse does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no objectionable content and is rated 4+.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version at this URL with an updated effective date.
